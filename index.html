        Integrity & Format
        
Official Rules & Offline Voting

        
Simple, transparent guidelines focused purely on artistic vision.

      


      

        
        

          

            
          

          
Participation Guidelines

          
    
            
  • 
              01.
              Open to Everyone: Photographers of any background, device, or skill level may participate.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              02.
              Maximum 3 Topics: Out of the 6 topics (Nature, Astrophotography, Miniatures, Urban, Structures, Random), select a maximum of 3 topics.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              03.
              1 Picture Per Chosen Topic: Submit only 1 photograph per chosen category (up to 3 photographs total per person).
            
    • 
            
  • 
              04.
              Original Capture Required: All submitted photographs must be taken by you.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              05.
              Edited Images Allowed (Must Be Tagged): Post-processed or edited photographs are welcome, provided you explicitly tag/label the entry as EDITED when sending it to zenwarrior_ht3 or epicmaster__.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              06.
              No Monetary or Physical Rewards: This is a pure passion and recognition showcase—no prizes or cash rewards will be given.
            
    • 
          

        


        
        

          

            

              
            

            
Offlineways Voting & Honors

            

              To eliminate online bot manipulation and public popularity bias, all judging for THE FRAME 2026 is conducted strictly through Offlineways—dedicated offline voting and external review outside this website.
            

            

              
Category Distinction

              

                From each of the 6 categories, 1 photograph will be selected through offline voting and officially announced on 1 January 2027 as Best in Category.
